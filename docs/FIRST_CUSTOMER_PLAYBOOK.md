# First Customer Operating Playbook

## Goal

Deliver the full promise before every workflow is automated.

## 1. Owner Rules Interview

Capture:
- services offered / refused
- service area
- business hours
- urgent call definition
- transfer rules
- scheduling rules
- quote/price authority
- labor/equipment billing rates
- material markup
- purchasing thresholds
- preferred/blocked vendors
- substitution policy
- overtime approval
- invoice approval
- AR reminder tone/cadence
- deposit/progress billing rules
- sensitive customers
- who may authorize what

## 2. Data import

Minimum:
- employees
- active customers
- active jobs
- vendors
- equipment
- service/rate basics
- existing accounting integration

## 3. Week-one service

Turn on:
- call overflow
- lead capture
- crew updates
- purchasing requests
- invoice-draft preparation
- receipt organization
- morning brief

## 4. Internal fulfillment matrix

For every workflow track:
- AI completed
- AI + human reviewed
- human completed
- failed / returned to owner

## 5. Daily ops review

Internal team reviews:
- open exceptions
- approvals aging
- failed provider calls
- unclear field updates
- call transfers
- invoice drafts
- purchasing requests

## 6. Weekly customer review

Show:
- calls handled
- leads captured
- owner hours saved estimate
- invoices prepared
- unbilled work discovered
- AR action
- purchase price checks
- supplier savings
- exception categories

Do not overwhelm with AI metrics.

## 7. Case-study target

After 30 days create:
- admin hours before/after
- calls answered
- leads
- invoice lag reduction
- unbilled revenue found
- purchasing time saved
- AR collected
- quote response speed
